# CSCI 441/541 Research Assignment (verbatim)

Source: `Week 7 up/CSCI 441 Research Assignment.docx` in the course materials folder (identical to the copy supplied separately). Total points: 10. Due date: check D2L — not stated in the document itself.

> Objective: To research the implementation of convolutional neural networks to do object detection/segmentation/classification
>
> For this assignment, you will need to do the following:
> - Identify a domain for this research assignment.
> - Identify the research focus, i.e., object detection, segmentation, or classification.
> - Either construct a training data set from scratch or use the existing data set from sources like GitHub, Kaggle, or online repository.
> - Research a technique to train a convolutional neural network on the data set you identified in step 3.
> - Test your trained model with a new unseen data and report the output of your model.
>
> Report:
> You will need to turn this assignment before the due date. Your assignment should include the following:
> - Your response to step 1 above (i.e., the domain you identified for this assignment)
> - Discuss your research focus and reasoning for its selection.
> - Describe your data set, i.e., provide details about the number of data points, size, dimensions, etc., applicable to your data set.
> - For step 4, it is recommended that you include your code that implements the training of a convolutional neural network. You can include the output/snapshot of the run of your model in this file.
> - Discuss the results that your model produced for any unseen data used to test it.
